# Topical Authority — reklame.bengkel-las.co.id

## Role and boundary

`reklame.bengkel-las.co.id` is a specialist Indonesian knowledge and commercial-support property for planning, selecting, producing, installing, and maintaining physical and digital outdoor advertising and business-signage systems. The knowledge layer should help business owners, brand teams, property managers, and procurement teams make informed decisions; existing catalog, contact, and product/service routes retain transactional intent. Regulatory, tax, structural, work-at-height, and electrical guidance must be verified against current official requirements and reviewed by a competent practitioner before publication. That publication gate does not materially change this lean taxonomy.

## Internal-link rule

Each article links upward to its RKL topic hub. Orientation pages in RKL-01 link to the relevant product hubs; RKL-02 briefs feed RKL-03/RKL-04 and the chosen product topic; product selection pages link forward to RKL-13, RKL-14, RKL-15, and RKL-16 only where relevant. Diagnostic pages link back to the owning product guide and onward to repair-versus-replacement guidance. Commercial calls to action point to the existing `/katalog/`, appropriate product/service route, or `/kontak/`; knowledge articles do not compete with those routes for quotation intent.
